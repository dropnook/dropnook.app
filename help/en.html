Text and files between all devices in your home network. No login, no accounts. Everyone sees the same thing, instantly.

Uploading files
Drag a file anywhere into the window or press “Upload”. Several at once work too.
Screenshots and pictures
Take a screenshot into the clipboard and press Ctrl+V here (⌘V on a Mac) — it is uploaded at once. Pictures show a small preview: click it to see the picture large. “Copy” next to it puts the picture itself into this computer's clipboard (works over HTTPS).
Whole folders
Do not work here — zip them first.
Mac: right-click the folder → “Compress …”. The .zip appears next to it.
Windows: right-click → “Send to” → “Compressed (zipped) folder”.
Then upload the ZIP file. Whoever needs it downloads it and unpacks it themselves. Zipping needs about as much free space on your computer as the folder is large.
Downloading
Right-click the file; on phones and tablets use the ⋮ button. For several: tick them, then “Download”. The browser may ask once whether it may save several files.
Large files
Are sent in 64 MB chunks and resume by themselves after an interruption — even if you pick the same file again later. Keep the tab open meanwhile.
Text fields
Formatting is dropped on purpose when pasting; plain text remains. “Copy” takes the whole field at once. The top of each field shows words and characters.
If the fields are not enough, there is “+ Text field” below them; × removes one again — for everyone. “Clear text fields” empties everything and goes back to the original number. If someone else writes at the same time, nothing is pulled away under your fingers — you get a note and decide yourself.
Links
If a web address appears in the text, it shows up below the field as a clickable button and opens in a new tab. The text field itself stays plain text — nothing is converted or stored in addition.
Sharing on the internet
When sharing is turned on, every text field has a “Share” button and every file has “Share on the internet…” in its menu. You choose how long the link is valid and pass it on. Whoever has it sees exactly that one text or downloads exactly that one file — without logging in, and nothing else from here.
Optionally with a password — then the page asks for it before showing anything. Send it through another channel than the link.
A text is shared as it stands when you click; later changes are not included. Deleting a file ends its link immediately.
“Shares” at the top lists all open links, how often they were opened, and lets you end each one right away.
Whatever is reachable from the internet always carries an orange “Public” badge — on the text field and on the file — and the “Shares” button turns orange. Anything without a badge is only visible here in the LAN.
Users and colours
Whoever set up Drop can give up to five people a name, a PIN and a colour of their own. Everyone then picks their name once per device. At the top you switch between your own area — only you see it — and the shared one that everyone sees; a dot there means something new. “Sign out” switches to someone else.
Without users, the round button at the top picks the colour for this browser: teal, gold, blue, violet or coral, each in light and dark.
When the connection drops
The dot at the top left turns red. You can keep typing: unsaved text stays on your device and is sent as soon as the connection is back.
Deleting
There is no recycle bin. Gone is gone — and gone for everyone.
Phones and tablets
Work, but with limits — be careful with very large files.
